        What you can do

          Safer buying habits

          • Pay with a credit card or a platform's buyer protection whenever you can.
          • Avoid gift cards, crypto, and bank transfers to people you have not verified.
          • Search the shop name plus the words "scam" or "reviews" before buying.
          • Check that the account has a history of real posts and real customers.
          • If something feels rushed, take a break. Scammers rely on urgency.
          This tool gives general guidance based on common warning signs. It cannot confirm whether a shop is legitimate. When in doubt, do not pay.
